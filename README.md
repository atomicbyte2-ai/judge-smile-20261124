## The entry

> The street cat I feed finally let me touch her head today — then stole my sandwich and sprinted off like she had planned it all week.

133 characters. One line about a small good thing that happened today.

## Why this line?

Warmth first: a shy street cat finally trusting a hand enough to be touched. Then the surprise turn: her thank-you is a sandwich heist, sprinting off like she had planned it all week. One concrete detail — the stolen sandwich — keeps it real instead of sentimental, and the wording is its own, no greeting-card phrasing.

## How to run

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```

Output: the line above, printed to stdout and written to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline, exactly as the judge harness reads it.
